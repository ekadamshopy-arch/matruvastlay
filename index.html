Who We Are

Driven by empathy, grounded in grassroots action.

Founded in 2019, Aarambh Foundation operates on a simple belief: poverty should never steal a child's right to learn and grow. We collaborate directly with village panchayats, parents, and government schools to build lasting educational infrastructure.

🎯

Our Mission

To eliminate digital illiteracy in rural schools and reduce the school dropout rate among young girls to zero by 2030.

👁️

Our Vision

An inclusive society where geography and financial background never dictate a child’s capacity to achieve their potential.

🛡️

100% Transparency

Every single rupee/dollar is accounted for. We publish audited financial balance sheets and project impact assessments bi-annually.
